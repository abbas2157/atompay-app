Before it can be built

  • Licence: Google Play needs proof of SECP approval to offer or facilitate lending in Pakistan.
  • APR: if the 3% fee is charged, disclose 18.5%, not just "0% markup".
  • KFS: shown at Atomshop checkout. The app is English only; SECP also asks for Urdu.
  • API: quote is wrong; no areas, products, KFS, complaints or Ask Atom endpoints.
  • Logo: a 1024 px or vector mark for the iOS icon.
