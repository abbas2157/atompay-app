Photograph your CNIC

AtomPay needs the camera to take your CNIC photos and a selfie.

  • Used only while you take the photo.
  • Photos go straight to verification and are not saved on your phone.
  • We never open your gallery.

The camera is off for AtomPay

To photograph your CNIC, turn it on in Settings › Apps › AtomPay › Permissions › Camera.

Can't use the camera? Contact us from Help and we will tell you what else we can accept.
